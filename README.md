# Accelerated Computing Research Lab (ACCL)

Welcome to the **Accelerated Computing Research Lab (ACCL)** – a premier research hub dedicated to advancing the frontiers of high-performance computing, graph analytics, machine learning, deep learning, and GPU architecture optimisation.

## 🎯 Our Mission

We strive to solve large-scale, real-world problems by building intelligent, scalable, and efficient systems for next-generation computing. Our work pushes the boundaries of what's possible in computational performance and efficiency.

## 🔬 Research Areas

Our research spans multiple cutting-edge domains:

- **High-Performance Computing (HPC)**: Developing advanced algorithms and systems for extreme-scale computational challenges
- **Graph Analytics**: Creating innovative solutions for large-scale graph processing and analysis
- **Machine Learning & Deep Learning**: Building efficient ML/DL frameworks and optimizing model training and inference
- **GPU Architecture Optimisation**: Maximizing performance through low-level hardware optimization and architecture design

## 🚀 What We Do

- **Research & Innovation**: Conducting fundamental and applied research in accelerated computing
- **System Development**: Building scalable, efficient systems for next-generation computing workloads
- **Performance Optimization**: Optimizing algorithms and architectures for maximum computational efficiency
- **Real-World Applications**: Applying our research to solve practical, large-scale problems

- Students and researchers passionate about accelerated computing
